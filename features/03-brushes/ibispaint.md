# ibisPaint: brush library and management

Source: ibispaint.com/lecture (№104, 137, 152, 155, 175, 200), app store listing.

- Library: 15000+ brushes, mostly online content.
  - Categories: dip pen, felt tip, digital pen, airbrush, fan, flat, pencil, oil, charcoal, crayon, stamps.
  - Chained brushes and lace, stamp brushes.
- Brush Window:
  - category tabs + **Custom**;
  - **Search** by name;
  - a parameters panel (the `>` button).
- **Custom brush**:
  - "+" on a base brush → a copy in Custom → edit → `<` saves;
  - Edit: order (drag) and Delete;
  - Reset returns it to the state it had when added.
- **Export/Import via QR code** (an image into the photo album). Sharing on social networks with #ibispaint.
- **Publishing to the Online Gallery**: a QR is inserted into an artwork, the artwork is uploaded, and the brush appears in the gallery with a delay.
- Online library: patterns are downloaded on demand. Settings → "Delete the Brush Pattern images" (cache).
- **Original brush patterns** (your own pattern): see 04 ibispaint.md.

For the clean room: QR exchange of brushes is a good idea for cross-device sharing. We make our own format + optional QR export.
